Main subject is a long supple dark animal crawling across the slab, clearly a coherent creature with velvety charcoal layered skin, subtle flexible segments, delicate short gripping limbs, many extremely fine tactile palps clustered around a narrow slit mouth, and several sensitive accordion-like folds along both flanks. No human face, no humanoid form, no robot, no monster teeth spectacle. Its fine palps carefully follow the same shallow engraved channel toward a small ceramic valve plate, illustrating patient examination before interruption. Flank folds show a progressive sequence of contractions. Main focus is anatomical and tactile, not magical spectacle. No black annihilation aura, no disappearing matter, no destructive energy beams, no floating symbols. In background a separate stitched wall protection seal with physical anchors and braided cord is visible, intact, hinting at its next target. Creature looks strange, beautiful, morally neutral, probing rather than attacking. Worn stone, glazed ceramic, ash, tactile believable surfaces, warm residual firelight against cool dark body, finely detailed palps in sharp focus, shallow cinematic depth of field but full animal silhouette readable.
