## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a cinematic photoreal painterly illustration of an original medieval fantasy creature called a RETOUR DE REGARD, an instinctive non-living animal of divination. Scene: abandoned stone courtyard, a weathered wooden cargo cart carrying bundled sacks and crates. Perched prominently on its load is a compact coherent animal on long delicate jointed legs. Its central body carries overlapping organic translucent shallow cup-shaped sensory organs like nacreous petals, some folded tightly and others unfurled. No humanoid face, no giant eyeball monster, no robot, no modern screens. The cups have physical curved pearly rims and watery translucent interiors. Critical visual story: TWO open cups show faint but recognizable FRAGMENTS OF A DISTANT ROOM that is NOT the courtyard. One larger cup shows only the edge of a wooden desk with a small medieval divination instrument; a second cup shows only a human hand wearing a simple distinctive engraved ring on that same desk. Images contained within curved cup interiors, imperfect and partially cloudy like biological scrying surfaces, not holograms or flat monitors. Most other cups remain milky and unreadable. Creature is turning its opened cup array directly toward the viewer, as if responding to being watched remotely. In deeper background, a discreet medieval robed human supervisor stands beside the cart, observing the cup surfaces, face secondary and softly out of focus. No telepathy effects. Make the creature occupy most of foreground with full silhouette and thin gripping feet visible, two readable cup fragments large enough to inspect. Natural cool overcast courtyard light, warm muted room colors inside cups, tactile wet translucent membranes, dusty wood, cloth, stone. Beautiful unsettling morally neutral creature, thoughtful restrained high-end fantasy bestiary art, landscape composition. No captions, no text, no floating symbols, no magic beams, no watermark. Proposed visual anatomy.
